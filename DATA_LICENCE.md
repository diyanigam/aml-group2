The bundled Burgers subset is derived from PDEBench Datasets, Takamoto et al., https://doi.org/10.18419/darus-2986 . The official record identifies CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . This kit selects trajectories and times and subsamples spatial grids; the original values are preserved. See .audit/benchmark_transformation.md for the selection and retained hashes.
